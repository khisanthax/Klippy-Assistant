Klippy uses unmodified Qt for Python community binaries under the applicable LGPL terms.
The application uses Qt Core, Gui, Widgets and Network as shared libraries. These libraries
and their Python bindings remain separate files, not statically linked into Klippy.exe.

You may replace the LGPL libraries with compatible modified versions for your own use,
and debug such modifications. No Klippy restriction prevents reverse engineering for
debugging changes to those libraries. Quit Klippy, back up the installation, and replace
the corresponding DLL/PYD files under _internal/PySide6 and _internal/shiboken6 with
ABI-compatible builds. Keep the matching Qt platform plugins. No signature enforcement
or app checksum lock prevents such replacement.

Upstream source for the exact initial binaries:
https://download.qt.io/official_releases/QtForPython/pyside6/PySide6-6.12.0-src/
https://download.qt.io/official_releases/qt/6.12/6.12.0/submodules/

Include the applicable LGPLv3/GPLv3 texts and notices with redistribution. The build's
licenses/inventory.json lists installed dependency versions and collected notices.
Model licenses are separate and the engineering preview does not distribute model weights.
Final corresponding-source and binary-license completeness remains a stable release gate.
