Klippy Assistant 0.6.1-preview.1 — Follow-up Conversation Engineering Preview

This package is unsigned and is not a stable v1.0 release.

It includes the desktop app, manual commands, safety confirmations, macro review and
notification-only printer monitoring. It includes local speech and language models;
no cloud service or post-install model download is required. Enable listening to load
the bundled engines. The microphone starts off unless configured otherwise.

The floating orb visualizes the existing microphone/playback streams and pending reviews.
Configure size, position, pinning, labels and reduced-motion behavior under Settings.
Desktop DPI/auto-hide/hot-plug acceptance remains open; see PREVIEW6_REPORT.md.

Hardware actuation and broad microphone/room accuracy have not been qualified. Test safely with a
simulated printer first. Firmware and physical emergency controls remain essential.

Settings and Windows credential-vault entries are preserved on upgrade and uninstall.
See README.md for backup, reset, data removal and known limitations.
